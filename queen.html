THE GAME

Croquet, Properly

equipment alive, rules optional

The mallets are live flamingoes who will not straighten their necks; the balls are live hedgehogs who unroll and trot away; the arches are soldiers who keep wandering off. Alice manages one stroke by tucking her flamingo under her arm — and the Duchess walks her round for morals.

Meanwhile the gardeners — Two, Five and Seven of Spades — are caught painting white roses red, and the Queen's verdict on everything, including Alice's neck, is immediate and identical.

♥ ♥ ♥

THE TRIAL

Who Stole the Tarts?

the Knave, the verse, the verdict

The King presides in a wig, the White Rabbit trumpets, the jurors scribble names for fear of forgetting them — and the whole case rests on a verse the Knave insists he didn't write, in handwriting that isn't his.

Alice, grown to full size at last, upsets the jury-box, contradicts the King to his face, and delivers the closing argument of the century:

THE DEFENCE RESTS

“You're nothing but a pack of cards!”

SENTENCE FIRST — VERDICT AFTERWARDS

…and the whole pack rose up into the air and came flying down upon her — and she woke, with leaves on her face, on the bank beside her sister.
