Alice

HEROINE · LOVER OF NONSENSE

Seven-ish, literal-minded, and braver than the whole court. Falls down holes, drinks from unlabelled bottles, and still corrects everyone's grammar on the way down.

Curiouser and curiouser!

White Rabbit

HERALD · CHRONICALLY LATE

Waistcoat, watch, and not a moment to spare. Mistakes Alice for his housemaid Mary Ann, serves the Duchess, and runs the whole story simply by being followed.

Oh dear! Oh dear! I shall be late!

Queen of Hearts

MONARCH · CROQUET ENTHUSIAST

Shouts first, thinks never. Her court paints roses, loses heads (nearly), and plays croquet with live equipment. Alice's verdict stands: a pack of cards.

Off with their heads!

Mad Hatter

HOST · STUCK AT SIX O'CLOCK

Quarrelled with Time and lost: it is always tea-time, the table is always full, the riddles never get answers. Price of his hat, as labelled: ten shillings sixpence.

Why is a raven like a writing-desk?

Caterpillar

ORACLE · HOOKAH DEVOTEE

Three inches high, sitting on a mushroom, asking the only question that matters. Dispenses one useful fact — one side makes you grow — then leaves.

Who are YOU?

Duchess

MORALIST · PEPPER ENTHUSIAST

Nurses a baby that becomes a pig, sneezes through her own kitchen, and finds a moral in absolutely everything — usually about pepper, mustard, or flamingos.

Everything's got a moral, if only you can find it.

Mock Turtle

STORYTELLER · SOUP DESTINY

Educated at sea — Reeling, Writhing, Uglification and Derision. Weeps beautifully, dances the Lobster Quadrille, and will one day be mock-turtle soup.

We called him Tortoise because he taught us.

Dormouse

NARRATOR · TREACLE-WELL HISTORIAN

Asleep in the teapot between stories of three sisters — Elsie, Lacie and Tillie — living at the bottom of a treacle-well on treacle. Pinched awake at intervals.

…three little sisters… learning to draw…

Knave of Hearts

DEFENDANT · TART SUSPECT

Stands trial for stolen tarts on the flimsiest of verses. Trembles, drops his crown (not his), and owes his acquittal to a girl who outgrew the courtroom.

…I didn't!…
